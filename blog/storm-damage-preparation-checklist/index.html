Storm Preparation for Homeowners in Camden, NJ

Home / Blog / Storm Damage Preparation Checklist in Camden, NJ

In Camden, NJ, a storm can leave homes and businesses with devastating water damage. You can greatly limit what a storm does to your property, and the restoration bill that follows, by preparing once before the season starts and again as each storm approaches. This checklist from Camden Restoration Co covers everything Camden homeowners should do to prepare.

Pre-Season Preparation

Ahead of the season, do the following: check the roof for flashing or shingles that are missing, damaged, or worn out. Clean gutters and downspouts thoroughly and ensure proper discharge. Look over every door, window, and exterior penetration, and seal around each one. Inspect your sump pump and test the backup power system. Review your insurance coverage including flood and wind damage. For insurance records, photograph the property as it is now. Trim trees near your home to reduce wind-driven branch damage.

When a Storm Is on the Way

Once a storm is forecast, tie down loose outdoor items before they can turn into projectiles. Shut the storm shutters, or put boards over windows, when the forecast warrants it. Confirm that the sump pump runs and that its backup battery is charged. Put important documents and valuables in waterproof containers or carry them to an upper level. Have supplies on hand and be sure you know the route you would use to evacuate. Program Camden Restoration Co's emergency number ((386) 349-5085) into your phone.

After the Storm Ends

Once it has passed, begin by inspecting the property from somewhere safe, and do not go in until you have. Document all damage with photos and video. Holes in the roof should be covered with a tarp, but only when that is safe. Electrical hazards must be assessed before anyone steps into a flooded area.
