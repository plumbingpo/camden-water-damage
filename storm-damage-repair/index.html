When to Call for Storm Damage Repair in Camden

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Rain blown in around door frames, window frames or openings in the wall
  • Ceilings or walls that stain while it is storming or soon afterward
  • Puddles near skylights, beside a chimney or on the attic floor
  • Downspouts knocked off and gutter runs hanging after high wind
  • Swollen ceiling panels or blistered, peeling paint in the days after a storm
  • A basement or ground floor that floods when the drains cannot keep up
  • Missing, damaged or lifted shingles you can see from the ground

Maintenance Tips for Storm Damage Repair

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Ice dams form when attics run warm, so check that yours has enough insulation and airflow
  • Branches break in high wind, so keep nearby trees pruned away from the roof and siding
  • Have your roof checked annually and fix vulnerable areas ahead of the stormy months
  • Cracked caulk lets blown rain in, so inspect and renew the seals on doors and windows
  • For insurance purposes, keep a yearly set of pictures showing the state of your property
  • Runoff should flow away from your foundation, so fix low spots and add downspout extensions
